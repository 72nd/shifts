Shift planning for events.

A free tool to coordinate who covers which shift - no accounts needed for participants.

How it Works

  1. Create a Plan

    Create the plan, then receive the URL and an admin password from the app.

  2. Add Shifts

    Add shift types and individual shifts with time, duration, and headcount.

  3. Share & Invite

    Share the link with participants. They open it, enter their name, and sign up.

  4. Manage & Monitor

    Lock shifts, track fill status, and export data from the same URL.

Two ways to look at your plan

List view

Shifts grouped by day and type. Expand a row to see participants, fill status, and details.

Timetable

A visual timeline of all shifts in a day. Useful for spotting gaps or overlaps at a glance.

Good to know

  • Free and open source.
  • Participants need no account - just a name.
  • Your admin password controls administrative actions.
  • Collaborative mode lets participants express interest while admins confirm assignments.
  • Export as CSV, PDF report, PDF timetable, or a printable poster.
  • Statistics view shows hours per person, fill rates, and participant collisions.
  • Bilingual plans (German and English) are supported.
  • REST API available for automation and integrations.

Joining an Existing Plan?

Open the link directly. If you do not have it, ask whoever is organizing the event.
