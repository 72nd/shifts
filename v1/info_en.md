**How does it work?** Welcome to the Shift Planner! Here you can sign up for various helper shifts.

1. **Select a shift**: Browse through the different shift types and find a suitable shift
2. **Sign up**: Click "Join" and enter your name
3. **Done**: You are now scheduled for the shift

**Important Notes**

- Please only sign up for shifts that you can definitely commit to
- When signing up, please use your full name or a unique nickname
- If you cannot make a shift after all, please remove yourself well in advance
- If you have any questions, please contact the shift planning coordination team

**Colour Legend**

- 🔴 Red: Critical staffing (≤ 50% of required helpers)
- 🟡 Yellow: Spots still available
- 🟢 Green: Fully staffed

**Filters**

You can filter the shifts by various criteria:
- By shift type
- By day
- By status (empty/critical/spots available/full)

Thank you for your support! 🙏
